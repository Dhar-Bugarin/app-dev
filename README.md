My Favorite Series: Anne with an E

About the Series

Anne with an E is a drama series about a young orphan girl named Anne Shirley who finds a new home in Green Gables. The story follows her journey as she discovers friendship, family, love, and her own identity.

Main Characters

- Anne Shirley – A creative, imaginative, and talkative girl.
- Diana Barry – Anne's loyal best friend.
- Gilbert Blythe – A kind and intelligent student.
- Marilla Cuthbert – One of Anne's guardians.
- Matthew Cuthbert – Marilla's kind-hearted brother.

Why I Love This Series

I love this series because it teaches important lessons about acceptance, kindness, friendship, and believing in yourself. Anne's imagination and positive outlook remind me that being different is not something to be ashamed of.
